Overview. The international SWISS DETOX store already looks and feels like a luxury brand, and it gets many European details right that other stores miss. Every price shows the unit price per litre and "Incl. VAT, free shipping worldwide". Product pages state a delivery time. The legal set is complete (Imprint, 14-day right of withdrawal with model form). The checkout charges in euros in the EU and in Swiss francs in Switzerland, and offers local methods such as MobilePay. On top of that come a sticky header, predictive search with prices, six site languages, live chat, a loyalty programme and Google Analytics 4 with Google Ads tracking. The brand also owns strong proof: 26 stored customer reviews averaging 5.0★, the Switzerland Prestige Awards "Luxury Skincare Brand of the Year" title, a dermatological study on 50 volunteers, and stockists from a Munich boutique to an international pharmacy.

Where the sales are lost: the path to the product. On desktop, clicking "SHOP" opens the homepage. The homepage itself is 13 sections and about 7,700 px of brand story without a single product or price. The hero button "Experience Swiss Detox" leads to a science page that has no link at all, and the FAQ ends the same way. The SHOP menu lists only 4 of the 9 products and leaves out the €25 Discovery Set, the natural first purchase in a €185–€755 range.

The international checkout path has avoidable stops. The "Check out" button stays disabled until a small "I accept the Terms and Conditions" box is ticked, and nothing tells the shopper why. The site promises "complimentary worldwide shipping" and the FAQ says "We deliver worldwide", yet the United Kingdom, Norway, Bulgaria and other countries can't be selected at checkout. The shipping policy lists Klarna, but none of our test checkouts (Finland, Germany, Switzerland) offered it. Product pages say "3 to 15 days" although EU orders arrive in 3–5 business days. Two products change price with the visitor's VAT rate (the Ritual is €755 in Germany and €796,23 in Finland). There is no country or currency selector, and the German version still shows English buttons.

Proof that shoppers never see, and a slow mobile site. The reviews app (Judge.me) was removed, so no star or review appears anywhere, although 23 five-star reviews of The Serum are still stored. Next to "Add to bag" there is nothing about Swiss manufacturing, testing or secure payment, and the button disappears after the first scroll. Measured with Lighthouse from our server in the EU, the mobile homepage and product page score 63/100, with the main content appearing after 7.2–7.5 s. The hero video downloads in full (15.5 MB) on every homepage visit, and 192 country-flag images load for a list nobody sees.

Within the brand guidelines. Nothing here needs a redesign. Every recommendation reuses the current Luxe theme, its sections, fonts and sage-and-gold palette, plus settings in Shopify Markets and Payments: menu and link targets, a product row on the homepage, a trust strip, a sticky bar, accordions, a country selector and reconnecting the reviews app. In total the audit found 12 critical issues, 48 warnings and 63 things already done right. The Critical list alone (33.5 hrs) clears the buyer journey from the first click to a completed EU checkout.

Timeline. About 4–5 weeks with one developer: Critical fixes live in week 1, Medium in weeks 2–4, Low in week 5 (110.5 hrs in total, fixed price $4,420).
